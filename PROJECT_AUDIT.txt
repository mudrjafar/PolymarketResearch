WHY SIGNAL_ENGINE IS NOT ACTIVE
Diamond V3 consumes fresh flow_state.json directly. The old signal_engine only repackaged the same flow state into signals.json and could introduce stale duplication.

CRITICAL AUDIT FIXES
1. Corrected V2 BUY/SELL amount semantics. BUY makerAmount is collateral/USD and takerAmount is shares; SELL is the reverse.
2. Directional flow now keeps taker/aggressor OrderFilled events only, preventing normal maker+aggressor matches from cancelling/double-counting the same settlement.
3. Added V2 NegRisk exchange coverage.
4. Added active-market metadata refresh every five minutes.
5. Confirmations only increment on new trade evidence; repeated scans of unchanged data no longer produce fake confirmations.
6. FAR timing now uses exactly the flow_tracker boundary (>24h), so 67.7h is FAR and no false metadata conflict is created.
7. Entry Quality remains separate from Signal Quality. A high-price market can have excellent flow but still fail Diamond entry quality.
8. Huge cashflow is a separate attention signal and cannot override verification, entry, concentration or resolution gates.

CAUTION
- Paper current price is the latest observed blockchain fill, not a guaranteed executable CLOB quote.
- Gamma endDate is used as timing metadata; it is not treated as a guarantee of the written resolution rules.
- Paper TP/SL/time limits are test settings and are not historically calibrated yet.
